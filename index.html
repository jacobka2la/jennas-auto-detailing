Services

Everything your car needs to look and feel cleaner.

From crumbs and dust in hard-to-reach spots to exterior shine and protection, Jenna's Auto Detailing focuses on the details customers actually notice.

Full Interior Detail

  • Full vacuum of carpets, seats, trunk, and floor mats
  • Dashboard, center console, cupholders, and door panels cleaned
  • Plastics and vinyl cleaned and conditioned
  • Interior windows and mirrors cleaned
  • Seat cleaning or shampoo if needed
  • Floor mat cleaning
  • Dust and debris removed from vents and tight areas

Exterior Detail

  • Full hand wash
  • Wheel and tire cleaning
  • Tire shine
  • Exterior window cleaning
  • Drying and spray wax for added shine and protection
